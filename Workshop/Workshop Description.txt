This mod shows city vitals like water, electricity and indicates the supply state by colors and shows percentages.
It also contains an advanced warning system for problems with buildings and networks. No more need for browsing all over the map to find problems - all problems appear as warnings and an included problem panel shows all buildings and networks with current problems. The problem panel has an inbuild problem localizer and will take you to the culprit. 

You can use the toggles in the main menu to turn on/off the problems shown in the warning panel and simultaneously above buildings. But keep in mind - the actual problems remain! 

Furthermore, you can use it to display the city limits and statistics.

The information panel has a popup menu for the most important functions. 

All panels are draggable and redesigned.
